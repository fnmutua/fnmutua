# Felix Mutua, Ph.D., MISK

**Geospatial systems, mobile GIS and remote sensing**

I design and build national and county geospatial information systems, field-data tools and early-warning platforms. More than twenty years of work with governments, UN agencies and development programmes across Africa.

Co-founder and Director of GIS at [Advanced Geospatial Solutions](https://www.ags.co.ke/). Senior Lecturer, Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology. Ph.D. in Civil Engineering, The University of Tokyo (climate-impact assessment and extreme-event forecasting, Lake Victoria Basin).

Nairobi, Kenya · [felix.mutua@gmail.com](mailto:felix.mutua@gmail.com) · [ags.co.ke](https://www.ags.co.ke/)

## What I work on

- National GIS and management-information systems: PostgreSQL/PostGIS, GeoServer, GeoNode, web GIS
- Offline-first mobile GIS and field data collection: Android, SQLite, GPS, ODK Central, KoboToolbox
- Earth observation and climate-risk analytics: Google Earth Engine, Python, Sentinel-2, Landsat
- Early warning, Common Alerting Protocol, and last-mile dissemination (app, SMS, USSD)

## Open source

QGIS plugins for pulling survey data straight onto the map:

- [Connector for ODK](https://github.com/fnmutua/connector-for-ODK) — [QGIS plugin](https://plugins.qgis.org/plugins/connect_odk/). Connects to ODK Central, splits layers, and supports QA/QC and upload workflows.
- [Connector for Kobo](https://github.com/fnmutua/Connector-for-KobotoolBox) — [QGIS plugin](https://plugins.qgis.org/plugins/connect_kobo/). Downloads KoboToolbox submissions as map layers, with geometry detection and a searchable survey list.

Most project repositories are private and can be shared on request.
